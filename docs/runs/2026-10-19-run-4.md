- The past-kill skip never had to fire (max 0.552).

**2. The crowd penalty added nothing; replace it, don't tune it.**
- It hit the cap on 12 of 13 ideas.
- Model-memory competitor lists were wrong where it mattered: the 0-name idea died on `generic`, and a 6-name idea passed `generic`.
- Novelty weighting helped slightly: it put the only `generic` pass at the gate.

**3. Per-sector research doubled the signals ($1.65 vs $0.43) but mostly from vendor blogs.**
- About 27 of 31 web signals come from companies that already sell a fix.
- That steers ideas toward problems that are, by construction, already sold. One kill named a signal source (Park Medical Billing) as the competitor.

**4. Nothing is pitch-worthy.**
- The closest is **Repair-Request Clock & Habitability Proof File for small property managers**. It passed 5 of 6 checks and died only on `autonomy`, because legal clocks across many jurisdictions need judgement.
- Scoped to one rule set (for example, Ontario RTA maintenance timelines), it could be re-run as an owner idea.

**5. The next highest-leverage change is an owner decision: the generator and the gate disagree about buyer choice.**
- Three of the 4 `generic` kills say the difference was only the buyer or channel:
  - "selling this bundle through bookkeepers … is packaging"
  - "cross-client aggregation is a positioning difference"
  - "targeting property managers instead of investors is a positioning change"
- Yet ideate principle 3 ("the best buyer … may not be the obvious buyer") and v6's "a clearly different mechanism for a different buyer is fine" push exactly that.
- Options:
  - **(a)** Tell ideate that a new buyer for an existing product is not a differentiator: name the incumbent and the functional gap it leaves.
  - **(b)** Let the gate pass a distribution wedge when research shows the incumbents don't serve that buyer.
- Either way, the generator and gate should agree.

**6. Second priority: enforce operator voice in harvest.**
- Have extraction tag each signal as operator or vendor.
- Count only operator-voice signals toward the gate's evidence rule.
- Point research at owner communities and associations.
