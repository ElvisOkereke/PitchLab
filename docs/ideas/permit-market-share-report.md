# Permit-Based Market Share Report for Home-Service Marketing Agencies

Turn public building and mechanical permit filings into a monthly "jobs won vs. competitors by zip code" report that agencies give HVAC clients and use to decide where ad budget goes.

## Problem

Contractors can't see the work they're losing. Owners can't track revenue from calls that were never logged, and their own systems are often whiteboards and shared calendars, so nothing records the jobs that went to competitors. Agencies end up writing reports from clicks and calls only. That reporting takes 20–30 hours per client each month (according to agency-reporting vendors), and it still doesn't answer the owner's real question: "Am I winning more installs than the shop down the street, and where should my next dollar go?"

## Buyer

Small and mid-size marketing agencies that serve HVAC, plumbing and other home-service contractors. Each agency has many contractor clients, spends a lot of hours every month on manual reporting, and needs proof that its spend is working. One permit dataset per metro area covers all of an agency's clients in that area.

## Proposed service

Gather public permit records (mechanical and HVAC replacement, water heater, plumbing) from city and county open-data portals and permit search pages in US and Canadian metros. Standardize the contractor names and license numbers, assign each permit to a zip code and job type, and produce an automated white-label PDF and dashboard for each client. It shows the client's permit count compared with named competitors, share-of-installs trends, and zip codes where competitors are growing, which tells the agency where to move geo budget. Existing reporting tools only pull ad and analytics platform data; they don't look at permits, which are the public record of jobs actually done. The data is public, and the pilot is one metro area and a few agencies.
