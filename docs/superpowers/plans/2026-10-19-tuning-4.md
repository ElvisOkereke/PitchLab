# Tuning 4: Align Ideation and the Gate, Operator-Voice Evidence, Drop the Crowd Penalty

> For agentic workers: one implementer handles the whole plan, TDD (failing test first, capture RED), then one task review. No live LLM calls in tests.

**Goal:** apply the owner's decisions of 2026-10-19 after run 4 (`docs/runs/2026-10-19-run-4.md`, "Read" 1, 2, 3, 5, 6):
1. **Option (a):** ideation is told that a new buyer or channel for an existing product is NOT a differentiator. Each idea must name the incumbents it knows and the functional gap they leave, and the gate judges that claimed gap, so both stages apply the same bar.
2. **Remove the competitor penalty** from the screen (it hit its cap on 12 of 13 ideas). Keep the double weight on novelty.
3. **Operator-voice evidence:** extraction tags each signal as `operator`, `neutral` or `vendor`. Vendor signals (marketing blogs, case studies, product pages of companies already selling a fix) do not count toward the gate's evidence rule, and harvest research is steered to owner communities and associations.

**Owner-confirmed call to flag in the report:** "operator-voice only" is implemented as *operator + neutral* (trade associations, news, government or analyst sources count as independent evidence); only `vendor` is excluded. Counting operators alone would starve the evidence rule, which needs several distinct sites.

**Global constraints:** all earlier rulings bind:
- R9: no transforms in structured schemas.
- R13: escape at render.
- R15/R18/R19: untrusted text via `wrapUntrusted`/`capField`.
- R20: idempotent stages, per-item writes in a transaction.
- Banned sources via `isBannedSource`.
- Never touch gamersgetaway.ca.
- Model and promptVersion recorded on rows.
- Prompt changes are new versions; keep old files; update tests that assert versions.
- Migrations are additive only (the CLI applies them to the existing local DB on start).

### T2: Ideation names incumbents and the functional gap (option a)

- `src/pipeline/ideate.ts`: lens idea schema gains `knownIncumbents: z.array(z.string().max(120)).max(8)` and `functionalGap: z.string().max(600)`. They are stored on the idea row. The owner-idea path does NOT invent them: owner ideas store null.
- New prompt `prompts/ideate.v7.md` (copy v6, keep v1–v6). Add, in the style of the existing rules:
  - "A new buyer, channel, distribution wedge or packaging of an existing product is NOT a differentiator. If the only difference between your idea and something already sold is who buys it or how it is sold, do not propose it."
  - "For each idea, list the incumbents you know (check `<already-sold>` first, then your own knowledge) in `knownIncumbents` (empty only if you truly know of none), and state in `functionalGap` the specific function or capability they leave unserved. The gap must be about what the product does, not who it is for."
  - Keep every v6 rule (minSignals, prior ideas, already sold/covered, no benchmark words — the ideate-prompt test bans "lead", "contact form", "agency", "uptime" as whole words; do not introduce them).
- Each signal in `<signals>` carries its voice: `- url: …\n  voice: operator|neutral|vendor|unknown\n  quote: …`. The prompt requires each idea to be grounded in ≥ `minSignals` signals of voice `operator` or `neutral` (or `unknown`) from different sites, and says vendor signals may inform what already exists but never count as evidence of pain.
- `src/pipeline/idea-text.ts` `ideaBlock`: when present, append `Known incumbents: …` (each capped at 120, joined with "; ") and `Functional gap: …` (capped at 600). Absent (null) → omitted lines. This block feeds screen, gate and deep-case.
- **Tests:** schema accepts and rejects (more than 8 incumbents; gap over 600 chars); fields are stored; owner ideas store null; the rendered prompt contains the new rules and each signal's voice line; `ideaBlock` includes the lines when present and omits them when null; prompt-version assertion expects ideate.v7; the banned-word test passes for v7.

### T3: Gate judges the claimed gap (`src/pipeline/gate.ts`)

- New prompt `prompts/gate.v2.md` (copy v1, keep v1). In the `generic` check, add: "The idea claims the functional gap and incumbents shown in its description. Mark `generic` as failed when the competitor research shows an incumbent already provides that function, or when the claimed gap is only about who buys the product or how it is sold (a positioning, packaging or channel difference). Pass `generic` only when the research shows a real functional gap or no incumbent serving this function." For ideas without a claimed gap (owner ideas), judge on the competitor research alone, as before.
- The gate passes `ideaBlock(idea)` (now including incumbents and gap) in the idea block.
- **Evidence rule:** count only signals whose `voice` is `operator`, `neutral` or NULL (legacy). Exclude `vendor`. The evidence verdict reasoning lists what was excluded: e.g. `"3 independent sources: …; excluded 2 vendor sources"`.
- Reuse the existing `countEvidence(urls)`: add a thin wrapper `countEvidenceForSignals(rows)` that filters by voice and calls it, or extend it, keeping existing exports and tests working.
- **Tests:** vendor signals don't count (5 vendor-only signals → 0 units); operator + neutral + NULL do; mixed example exact reasoning text; gate.v2 loaded (prompt-version assertions updated); the gate prompt contains the new `generic` wording; owner idea without a gap still works.

### T4: Screen drops the crowd penalty (`src/pipeline/screen.ts`)

- Remove `crowdPenalty`, `ScreenScoresSchema` and the `knownCompetitors` field; screen uses `AiScoresSchema` again. Keep `weightedScreenTotal` (novelty counted double), now `(sum of the 7 criteria + novelty) / 8`.
- New prompt `prompts/screen.v3.md` (copy v1 semantics; keep v1, v2): no competitor list.
- Existing `ideas.screenScores` rows with `knownCompetitors` stay valid (jsonb).
- Remove or rewrite the tests that assert the penalty; keep: novelty weighting, skip rules (mechanism cap, near-twin, case similarity, killed similarity), owner ideas untouched. Update the shared test helper (`scores()`).
- **Tests:** ranking equals weighted score; equal raw scores tie by input order; novelty 5 vs 1 ranks first; screen prompt-version assertion expects screen.v3.

### T5: Harvest tags voice and steers to owner communities (`src/pipeline/harvest.ts`)

- New prompt `prompts/extract-signals.v3.md` (keep v1, v2). Each extracted signal gets `voice`:
  - `operator`: written by someone who runs, works in, or buys for the affected kind of business, describing their own situation (forum posts, reviews written by customers, owner blog posts, local-news interviews with owners).
  - `neutral`: an independent third party reporting on the pain: trade association, government or statistics body, analyst or journalist, academic source.
  - `vendor`: a company or consultant selling a product or service that addresses the pain (marketing blog, case study, product or pricing page, comparison page).
  - Keep the untrusted-data handling from v2.
- `extractSchema` gains `voice: z.enum(['operator','neutral','vendor'])`. Store it in `signals.voice`. Signals already in the DB (same url + quote hash) keep their existing row (`onConflictDoNothing`) — document this.
- New prompt `prompts/harvest-research.v5.md` (keep v1–v4). Add: "Prefer posts and pages written by owners and operators (forums, communities, subreddit-style discussion sites that are not banned, local news, owner interviews) and by neutral bodies (associations, regulators, statistics offices). Vendor blogs and product pages tell you what already exists, not how much the problem hurts: use them only to learn what is already sold, and expect them to be tagged as vendor." Same variables as v4.
- `ideate.ts` passes each signal's voice (`unknown` when NULL) as in T2.
- `export-markdown.ts`: each signal bullet shows `(voice: operator)` etc. when known.
- **Tests:** extraction schema requires voice; an extraction response with `voice:'vendor'` is stored as vendor; harvest-research prompt version assertion expects v5 and contains the new line; ideate prompt shows `voice: unknown` for legacy rows; export shows the voice label and omits it for NULL.
